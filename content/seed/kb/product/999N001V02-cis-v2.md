# Suraksha Term Shield: Customer Information Sheet v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Key features

DUMMY: Suraksha Term Shield, unique identification number 999N001V02, is a non-linked, non-participating individual pure risk life insurance plan. It pays a benefit only if the life assured dies during the policy term and pays nothing if the life assured survives to the end of the term. The table below summarises the main limits; the policy wording clause for each row is given in brackets.

| Feature | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Entry age | 18 to 65 years (clause 1) |
| Maximum age at end of cover | 85 years (clause 1) |
| Sum assured on death | 25 lakh to 10 crore rupees (clause 1) |
| Policy term | 10 to 40 years (clause 1) |
| Premium paying term | Regular, limited 10 years, or single premium (clause 1) |
| Death benefit payout | Lump sum or monthly income over 10 years (clause 2.2) |
| Riders | Accidental death benefit 999A007V01, waiver of premium 999A008V01, critical illness 999A009V01 |

The premium depends on the age, the sum assured, the policy term, the premium paying term, tobacco use and the riders chosen. Premiums shown before underwriting are indicative, and the final premium is fixed only after underwriting.

## 2. Benefits and exclusions in brief

DUMMY: On death during the policy term, the nominee receives the highest of the sum assured on death, ten times the annualised premium and one hundred and five percent of the premiums paid (clause 2.1). The benefit can be paid at once or as one hundred and twenty monthly instalments, as chosen at the start (clause 2.2). There is no maturity or survival benefit. The only exclusion on the base cover is suicide within twelve months of the start or revival of the policy, when eighty percent of the premiums paid or the surrender value, whichever is higher, is paid instead (clause 3.1). There is no waiting period on the base death benefit; rider waiting periods are in the rider wordings (clause 3.3). If a material fact is not disclosed in the proposal, a claim may be repudiated within three years under section 45 of the Insurance Act (clause 6.2), so answer every question fully and correctly. Riders have their own benefits and exclusions: for example, the accidental death benefit rider does not pay for death from illness, and the critical illness rider excludes pre-existing conditions for forty-eight months. Read each rider wording before choosing a rider. The plan offers no loan and pays no bonus, because it is a non-participating plan.

## 3. Your rights, service and claims

DUMMY: You have a free-look period of thirty days from receipt of the policy document to return the policy if you disagree with its terms; the premium is refunded less the risk premium for the period covered, stamp duty and medical costs (clause 4.1). A grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days for monthly premiums, applies to each premium; unpaid premiums after the grace period make a regular or limited pay policy lapse (clause 4.2). A lapsed policy may be revived within five years (clause 5.1). Regular pay policies have no surrender value; limited pay and single premium policies have a special surrender value (clause 5.2). To make a claim, contact any branch, the website, the app or the call centre; claims needing no investigation are settled within fifteen days of the last document (clause 6.1). For complaints, contact the grievance cell; if not resolved within fourteen days, escalate to the Grievance Redressal Officer, the Bima Bharosa portal or the Insurance Ombudsman. Keep the policy document, the premium receipts and this sheet in a safe place, and tell your nominee where to find them. You can update your nominee, address or contact details at any time through the app or a branch, free of charge.
