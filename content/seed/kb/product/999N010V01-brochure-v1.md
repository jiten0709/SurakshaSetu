# Suraksha Saver Guarantee: Brochure v1 (DUMMY)

## 1. Guaranteed savings with life cover

DUMMY: Suraksha Saver Guarantee helps you save for a goal such as a child's education or your retirement, with every benefit fixed on the day you buy the policy. You pay premiums for ten years, and your policy grows through guaranteed additions credited at the end of every policy year while you keep paying. At maturity, after a term of ten to twenty-five years, you receive the guaranteed maturity benefit plus the guaranteed additions, as a lump sum or as a fixed monthly income for five years. If you die during the term, your family receives the sum assured on death plus the guaranteed additions credited so far. The life cover ranges from five lakh to one crore rupees. The plan does not depend on market returns or bonus declarations, so what you see in your benefit illustration is what the policy pays if all premiums are paid. This product is seeded for testing and is not offered for sale through the assistant in phase 1. Because the benefits are fixed in rupees at the start, their value in future years is affected by inflation, so consider how much the maturity amount will buy when you need it. Premiums can be paid yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly for ten years.

## 2. Flexibility and what to watch for

DUMMY: After two full years of premiums, the policy acquires a guaranteed surrender value and you can take a loan of up to seventy percent of that value in an emergency. If you stop paying after two years, the policy becomes paid-up with reduced benefits and keeps the guaranteed additions already credited; if you stop earlier, the policy lapses and nothing is payable. You can revive a lapsed or paid-up policy within five years. The optional waiver of premium rider pays your remaining premiums if you become totally and permanently disabled or suffer a covered critical illness. Surrendering in the early years returns less than the premiums paid, so choose a premium you can keep paying for ten years. You have thirty days after receiving the policy to return it. Tax benefits depend on the income-tax law in force and may change. This brochure is a summary; read the benefit illustration, the customer information sheet and the policy wording before you buy. The death benefit is never less than one hundred and five percent of the premiums paid, and it is paid to your nominee as a lump sum. Claims can be made online, by phone or at any branch. Premiums paid may qualify for deduction under the old tax regime within the overall limit, subject to the law in force.
