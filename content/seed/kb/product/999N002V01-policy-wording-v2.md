# Suraksha Term Shield ROP: Policy Wording v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Definitions

DUMMY: In this policy, the life assured is the person whose life is insured and the policyholder is the owner of the policy, as named in the policy schedule. The sum assured on death is chosen at inception between twenty-five lakh rupees and two crore rupees. The policy term is chosen at inception between fifteen and thirty-five years, and the cover ends at the latest when the life assured reaches age seventy-five. The entry age of the life assured is between eighteen and fifty-five years last birthday. The premium paying term is either regular pay, for the whole policy term, or limited pay, for ten years. Total premiums paid means the total of all base premiums received, excluding taxes, rider premiums, modal loadings and any extra premium charged on underwriting. The maturity date is the last day of the policy term shown in the schedule. The nominee is the person named to receive the death benefit. A paid-up policy is one on which premiums stopped The annualised premium is the premium payable in a year under the chosen premium paying term, excluding taxes, rider premiums, underwriting extra premiums and modal loadings. The schedule is the document issued with this policy that records the life assured, the sum assured, the terms chosen, the premium and the return of premium basis. after the policy acquired a surrender value and which continues with reduced benefits under section 5.2.

## 2. Benefits

DUMMY: This plan pays a benefit on death during the policy term, and returns the total premiums paid if the life assured survives to the maturity date with the policy in force. It is a non-linked, non-participating plan and does not share in any profits.

### 2.1 Death benefit

DUMMY: On the death of the life assured during the policy term while the policy is in force, the death benefit is the highest of the sum assured on death, ten times the annualised premium, and one hundred and five percent of the total premiums paid up to the date of death. The death benefit is paid as a lump sum to the nominee, and the policy ends. No return of premium is payable after a death benefit has been paid.

### 2.2 Return of premium on maturity

DUMMY: If the life assured survives to the maturity date and all premiums due have been paid, the insurer pays one hundred percent of the total premiums paid as the maturity benefit, and the policy ends. The maturity benefit is paid as a lump sum only. The return of premium excludes taxes, rider premiums and extra premiums, so the amount received on maturity is less than the total amount the policyholder has paid over the term. For a paid-up policy, the maturity benefit is reduced in the proportion that premiums paid bear to premiums payable.

## 3. Exclusions and waiting periods

DUMMY: The benefits of this policy are subject to the exclusions and waiting periods in this section. No other exclusion or waiting period applies to the base cover, and each rider has its own exclusions and waiting periods set out in its rider wording.

### 3.1 Exclusions

DUMMY: If the life assured dies by suicide within twelve months from the date of commencement of risk or from the date of revival, the nominee receives the higher of eighty percent of the total premiums paid up to the date of death and the surrender value available on the date of death, provided the policy is in force. No other exclusion applies to the base death benefit: death from illness, accident, natural calamity or any other cause is covered from the date of commencement of risk. The accidental death benefit rider, if chosen, has its own exclusions, such as death from participation in hazardous sports, criminal acts or the influence of alcohol or drugs, which are set out in its rider wording. Non-disclosure of material facts in the proposal is dealt with under section 6.2 and section 45 of the Insurance Act, and is not an exclusion.

### 3.2 Waiting periods

DUMMY: The base death benefit has no waiting period, and cover for death from any cause other than suicide begins on the date of commencement of risk. The twelve-month suicide provision applies from commencement or from the date of any revival. The only rider available with this plan is the accidental death benefit rider, which has no waiting period but pays only if death occurs within one hundred and eighty days of the accident. The return of premium on maturity has no waiting period of its own, but it is payable only if the policy is in force on the maturity date with all premiums paid. Critical illness and waiver of premium riders are not available with this plan, so there is no illness waiting period to consider. A policy revived after lapse restarts the suicide period from the date of revival, and the insurer may require evidence of health as a condition of revival.

## 4. Free-look and grace period

DUMMY: This section sets out the policyholder's right to return the policy after receiving it, and the time allowed to pay each premium after its due date.

### 4.1 Free-look period

DUMMY: The policyholder may review the policy for thirty days from the date of receipt of the policy document. If the policyholder does not agree with any term, the policy may be returned within this period by a written request, through the website or through the app, stating the objection. The insurer will refund the premium within seven days, after deducting the proportionate risk premium for the period of cover, stamp duty and the cost of any medical examination. There is no surrender charge on a free-look cancellation, and the return of premium feature does not apply to a policy cancelled during free-look, because the refund is made instead. For a policy issued in electronic form, the free-look period starts on the date the electronic policy reaches the policyholder's registered email or account. A cancelled policy cannot be reinstated, and any new application is underwritten afresh.

### 4.2 Grace period

DUMMY: A grace period of thirty days is allowed after the due date of each yearly, half-yearly or quarterly premium, and fifteen days for each monthly premium. The policy remains in force with full cover during the grace period. If the life assured dies during the grace period, the death benefit is paid after deducting the unpaid premium. If a premium remains unpaid at the end of the grace period and the policy has not yet acquired a surrender value, the policy lapses and all cover ceases. If the policy has acquired a surrender value, it does not lapse but becomes paid-up automatically under section 5.2, with reduced benefits. The insurer sends a reminder before each due date, but the consequences of non-payment apply whether or not the reminder is received.

## 5. Revival and surrender

DUMMY: This section sets out how a lapsed or paid-up policy can be restored, and what the policyholder receives if the policy is surrendered or becomes paid-up.

### 5.1 Revival

DUMMY: A lapsed or paid-up policy may be revived within five years from the due date of the first unpaid premium and before the maturity date. The policyholder must apply for revival, pay all overdue premiums with interest at the revival rate declared by the insurer, and give any evidence of insurability the insurer asks for under its board-approved underwriting policy. On revival the original sum assured and the full return of premium benefit are restored from the date of revival. The insurer may decline or impose conditions on a revival, with reasons. The suicide provision applies again for twelve months from the date of revival. If a lapsed policy is not revived within five years it terminates without value; a paid-up policy that is not revived continues with its reduced benefits until maturity.

### 5.2 Surrender and paid-up value

DUMMY: The policy acquires a surrender value after premiums for two full policy years have been paid. The surrender value is the higher of the guaranteed surrender value and the special surrender value, calculated using the factors filed with the Authority and set out in the annexure. The guaranteed surrender value is a percentage of the total premiums paid that rises with the number of years the policy has run. If premiums stop after the policy has acquired a surrender value, the policy becomes paid-up: the sum assured on death and the maturity benefit are reduced in proportion to the premiums paid. The policyholder may surrender the policy at any time after it acquires a surrender value, and payment is made within seven days of a complete request. On surrender the policy and any rider end.

## 6. Claims and policy conditions

DUMMY: This section sets out how to make a claim and the legal conditions that apply to the policy throughout its term.

### 6.1 Claims

DUMMY: A death claim may be intimated by the nominee or legal heir through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre, with the claim form, the death certificate, the policy document, proof of identity and bank details of the claimant, and, for an unnatural death, the police and post-mortem reports. For a death within three years of commencement or revival, the insurer may ask for medical records to investigate. The claim is settled within fifteen days of the last document where no investigation is needed, and any investigation is completed within forty-five days of intimation. For the maturity benefit, the insurer writes to the policyholder at least sixty days before the maturity date asking for bank details and proof of identity, and pays on the maturity date. Late payment carries interest at two percent above the bank rate.

### 6.2 Misstatement, nomination and assignment

DUMMY: The policy is issued on the basis of the proposal. Under section 45 of the Insurance Act, it cannot be called in question on any ground after three years from the later of the date of issue, commencement of risk or revival; within three years it may be repudiated for fraud or for misstatement or suppression of a material fact, with written reasons. The policyholder may nominate under section 39 and assign under section 38 of the Insurance Act; an assignment cancels the nomination. The insurer records a nomination or assignment only on receipt of written notice. This plan offers no policy loan.
