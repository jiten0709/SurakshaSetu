# Suraksha Term Shield: Policy Wording v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Definitions

DUMMY: In this policy, the following words have the meanings given here. The life assured is the person whose life is insured, named in the policy schedule. The policyholder is the owner of the policy, who may be the life assured or a proposer with an insurable interest. The sum assured on death is the amount chosen by the policyholder at inception, between twenty-five lakh rupees and ten crore rupees, as shown in the schedule. The policy term is the period of cover, between ten and forty years, chosen at inception, and the cover ends at the latest at age eighty-five of the life assured. The premium paying term is the period for which premiums are payable: regular pay for the whole policy term, limited pay for ten years, or a single premium at inception. The nominee is the person named to receive the benefit on death. The date of commencement of risk is the date on which cover starts, shown in the schedule. A lapse means the policy has ended because a premium was not paid within the grace period, and revival means restoring a lapsed policy on the terms in section 5.1. Words in the singular include the plural The annualised premium is the premium payable in a year under the chosen premium paying term, excluding taxes, rider premiums, underwriting extra premiums and modal loadings. The schedule is the document issued with this policy that records the life assured, the sum assured, the terms chosen and the premium., and headings are for convenience only.

## 2. Benefits

DUMMY: This is a pure protection plan. It pays a benefit only on the death of the life assured during the policy term and pays nothing on survival to the end of the term.

### 2.1 Death benefit

DUMMY: On the death of the life assured during the policy term, while the policy is in force, the death benefit is the highest of the sum assured on death, ten times the annualised premium, or one hundred and five percent of the total premiums paid up to the date of death. For a single premium policy, the death benefit is the higher of the sum assured on death and one hundred and twenty-five percent of the single premium. The death benefit is paid to the nominee, or to the legal heirs if no nominee is alive, and the policy ends on payment. Premiums exclude taxes, rider premiums and any extra premium charged on underwriting.

### 2.2 Benefit payment options

DUMMY: At inception the policyholder chooses how the death benefit will be paid, and the choice cannot be changed later. Under the lump sum option, the whole death benefit is paid at once. Under the monthly income option, the death benefit is paid in one hundred and twenty equal monthly instalments over ten years, and the nominee may ask for the present value of the outstanding instalments to be paid at once, discounted at the rate stated in the schedule. The option chosen appears in the policy schedule.

## 3. Exclusions and waiting periods

DUMMY: The benefits of this policy are subject to the exclusions and waiting periods in this section. No other exclusion or waiting period applies to the base cover, and each rider has its own exclusions and waiting periods set out in its rider wording.

### 3.1 Suicide

DUMMY: If the life assured dies by suicide within twelve months from the date of commencement of risk or from the date of revival of the policy, the nominee receives the higher of eighty percent of the total premiums paid up to the date of death and the surrender value available on the date of death, provided the policy is in force. After twelve months no suicide exclusion applies. This is the only exclusion on the base death benefit of this plan.

### 3.2 What the exclusion does not cover

DUMMY: Death from any other cause, including illness, accident, natural disaster, pandemic or terrorism, is covered from the date of commencement of risk without any exclusion or waiting period. Death while travelling or living abroad is covered provided the premiums are paid. Non-disclosure of material facts is not an exclusion but is dealt with under section 6.2, which follows section 45 of the Insurance Act.

### 3.3 Waiting periods

DUMMY: The base death benefit of this plan has no waiting period: cover for death from any cause other than suicide begins on the date of commencement of risk, which is the date the insurer accepts the proposal and the first premium. The suicide provision in section 3.1 applies for twelve months from commencement or revival. Riders attached to this plan carry their own waiting periods: the critical illness rider has a ninety-day waiting period from commencement or revival for the diagnosis of a covered illness, and a survival period of thirty days after diagnosis; the waiver of premium rider applies a ninety-day waiting period for illness but none for accidental disability; the accidental death benefit rider has no waiting period but requires death within one hundred and eighty days of the accident. These rider periods are set out in full in the rider wordings, which form part of this policy when the rider is chosen. A policy revived after lapse starts the suicide period again from the date of revival, and the insurer may ask for evidence of health before revival.

## 4. Free-look and grace period

DUMMY: This section sets out the policyholder's right to return the policy after receiving it, and the time allowed to pay each premium after its due date.

### 4.1 Free-look period

DUMMY: The policyholder has a period of thirty days from the date of receipt of the policy document to review the terms and conditions of this policy. If the policyholder disagrees with any of the terms, the policy may be returned during this period with a written request or through the website or app, stating the objection. The insurer will cancel the policy and refund the premium received within seven days of the request, after deducting the proportionate risk premium for the period the life assured was covered, the stamp duty paid on the policy, and any expenses incurred on medical examination of the life assured. The free-look cancellation ends every rider attached to the policy. No surrender charge applies. Where the policy is issued in electronic form, the free-look period runs from the date the electronic policy is delivered to the policyholder's registered email or account. A policy cancelled during free-look cannot be reinstated; the customer may apply for a new policy, which is underwritten afresh at the then current age and premium rates.

### 4.2 Grace period

DUMMY: A grace period of thirty days is allowed for payment of each yearly, half-yearly or quarterly premium after its due date, and fifteen days for each monthly premium. During the grace period the policy stays in force with full cover, including any rider cover. If the life assured dies during the grace period, the death benefit is paid after deducting the premium due but not paid. If the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the regular pay and limited pay policy lapses, all cover ceases, and no benefit is payable. A single premium policy has no grace period because no further premium is due. The insurer will send a reminder before each due date and a notice after the grace period ends, but the policy lapses whether or not the notice is received. A lapsed policy may be revived under section 5.1.

## 5. Revival and surrender

DUMMY: This section sets out how a policy that has lapsed can be restored, and what the policyholder receives if the policy is surrendered.

### 5.1 Revival

DUMMY: A lapsed policy may be revived within five years from the due date of the first unpaid premium and before the end of the policy term. To revive the policy, the policyholder must apply in writing or through the app, pay all overdue premiums together with interest at the revival rate declared by the insurer, which is linked to the published benchmark stated in the schedule, and provide such evidence of continued insurability as the insurer may require under its board-approved underwriting policy. The insurer may accept the revival on the original terms, accept it with an extra premium, or decline it, and will give reasons for any decline. Revival takes effect from the date the insurer approves it, and the suicide provision in section 3.1 applies again from that date. Riders may be revived together with the base policy only. If the policy is not revived within five years, it terminates and no benefit is payable.

### 5.2 Surrender

DUMMY: A regular pay policy under this plan does not acquire any surrender value, and no amount is paid if it is discontinued. A limited pay or single premium policy acquires a surrender value once the first full year of premiums has been paid for limited pay, or immediately for a single premium. The surrender value is the special surrender value calculated as a percentage of the total premiums paid, which reduces over the remaining policy term, using the factors filed with the Authority and set out in the annexure to this policy. The policyholder may surrender the policy at any time by submitting a request with the policy document and proof of identity, and the surrender value is paid within seven days. On surrender the policy ends and all cover, including rider cover, ceases. A limited pay policy on which premiums stop before the first full year lapses without value. The insurer does not advise any policyholder to surrender a policy in order to buy another.

## 6. Claims and policy conditions

DUMMY: This section sets out how to make a claim and the legal conditions that apply to the policy throughout its term.

### 6.1 Claims

DUMMY: A death claim may be intimated by the nominee or legal heir through any branch, the website, the app, the call centre or email, as soon as possible after the death. The claimant should submit the claim form, the death certificate issued by the local authority, the policy document or an indemnity if it is lost, proof of identity and bank details of the claimant, and, where death was not due to natural causes, the police report and post-mortem report. For a death within three years of commencement or revival, the insurer may ask for medical records needed to investigate the claim. The insurer will settle a claim that needs no investigation within fifteen days of receiving the last document, and will complete any investigation within forty-five days of intimation. If a claim is paid late, interest is paid at two percent above the bank rate for the period of delay. A claim may be repudiated only on grounds in this policy, with written reasons.

### 6.2 Misstatement, nomination and assignment

DUMMY: This policy is issued on the basis of the answers given in the proposal. Under section 45 of the Insurance Act, the policy cannot be called in question on any ground after three years from the date of issue, commencement of risk or revival, whichever is later; within three years it may be repudiated on the ground of fraud, or of misstatement or suppression of a fact material to the risk, with written reasons and a refund of premiums where the law requires it. Nomination is made and changed under section 39 of the Insurance Act, and the policyholder may name one or more nominees at any time. The policy may be assigned under section 38, and an assignment cancels any nomination. Every change is recorded only after the insurer receives written notice.
