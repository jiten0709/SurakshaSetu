# Suraksha Saver Guarantee: Customer Information Sheet v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Key features

DUMMY: Suraksha Saver Guarantee, unique identification number 999N010V01, is a non-linked, non-participating individual savings life insurance plan with guaranteed benefits. The entry age is eighteen to fifty-five years, the policy term is ten to twenty-five years, and the policy matures by age eighty at the latest (clause 1). Premiums are payable for ten years only. The sum assured on death is between five lakh and one crore rupees. Guaranteed additions are credited at the end of each policy year for which premiums are paid. The maturity benefit can be taken as a lump sum or as a monthly income for five years. The only rider available is the waiver of premium rider 999A008V01. A benefit illustration showing the guaranteed benefits for your chosen premium is given with this sheet and must be acknowledged before the proposal. This plan is seeded for testing and is not offered for sale in phase 1. The benefit illustration shows the guaranteed additions and the maturity benefit year by year for the premium you choose, and these figures are guaranteed if all premiums are paid on time. Premiums can be paid yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly, and non-annual premiums carry modal factors. Because all benefits are fixed at the start, the plan pays no bonus.

## 2. Benefits and exclusions in brief

DUMMY: On death during the policy term, the nominee receives the sum assured on death plus the guaranteed additions accrued, subject to at least one hundred and five percent of the premiums paid (clause 2.1). On survival to maturity, the guaranteed maturity benefit plus the accrued guaranteed additions is paid as a lump sum or as a monthly income for five years (clause 2.2). All benefits are guaranteed; there are no bonuses and no non-guaranteed benefits. The only exclusion on the base cover is suicide within twelve months of the start or revival (clause 3.1). The waiver of premium rider has a ninety-day waiting period for illness (clause 3.2). Non-disclosure of a material fact may lead to repudiation within three years under section 45 of the Insurance Act (clause 6.2). Guaranteed additions accrue only for years whose premium is paid, and additions already credited are kept if the policy becomes paid-up. The monthly income option pays a fixed amount for sixty months, which in total is more than the lump sum because payment is deferred. The death benefit is never less than one hundred and five percent of the premiums paid.

## 3. Your rights, service and claims

DUMMY: You may return the policy within thirty days of receipt if you disagree with its terms (clause 4.1). Each premium has a grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days for monthly premiums (clause 4.2). If premiums stop in the first two years, the policy lapses without value; after two full years it becomes paid-up with reduced benefits and keeps the guaranteed additions already credited (clauses 4.2 and 5.2). Revival is possible within five years (clause 5.1). A guaranteed surrender value is available after two full years of premiums, and a loan of up to seventy percent of the surrender value may be taken (clauses 5.2 and 6.2). Claims are made through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre and are settled within fifteen days of the last document when no investigation is needed (clause 6.1). Complaints go to the grievance cell, then the Grievance Redressal Officer, Bima Bharosa or the Insurance Ombudsman. Keep the benefit illustration with the policy document, as it shows what the policy will pay. You can update your nominee, address and bank details through the app or a branch at no cost.
