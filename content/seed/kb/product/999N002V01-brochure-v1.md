# Suraksha Term Shield ROP: Brochure v1 (DUMMY)

## 1. Protection with your premiums back

DUMMY: Suraksha Term Shield ROP gives your family a lump sum if you die during the policy term, and gives you back the premiums you paid if you survive to the end of it. It suits people who want life cover but prefer not to feel that their premiums are lost if no claim is made. You choose a cover between twenty-five lakh and two crore rupees and a term between fifteen and thirty-five years, and the cover runs until age seventy-five at the latest. You can pay premiums for the full term or for ten years only. At the end of the term you receive one hundred percent of the base premiums paid, excluding taxes, rider premiums and any extra premium charged because of your health. Remember that the premium for this plan is higher than for a pure term plan with the same cover, because it funds the return of premium, and that the amount returned has no interest or growth added. Compare this plan with a pure term plan before you decide: the difference in premium, if invested elsewhere, may grow to more than the premium returned, but a return of premium plan suits those who value getting their premiums back without investing the difference themselves. Premiums can be paid yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly.

## 2. Things to know before you buy

DUMMY: The plan covers death from any cause from the first day, except suicide within the first twelve months, when a part of the premiums paid is returned instead. You can add the accidental death benefit rider for an extra amount on accidental death within one hundred and eighty days of the accident. If you stop paying premiums in the first two years your cover ends; after two years your policy continues as paid-up with reduced cover and a reduced return of premium, and you can revive it within five years. The plan acquires a surrender value after two full years of premiums, but surrendering early returns much less than the premiums paid. You have thirty days after receiving the policy to return it if you change your mind. Tax benefits, if any, depend on the income-tax law in force and your tax regime, and can change. This brochure is a summary: the policy wording prevails, and the customer information sheet sets out the key terms. The death benefit is paid to your nominee as a lump sum. You can update your nominee and contact details at any time through the app or a branch. Claims can be intimated online, by phone or at any branch, and a claim needing no investigation is settled within fifteen days of the last document.
