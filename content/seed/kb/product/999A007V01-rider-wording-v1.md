# Suraksha Accidental Death Benefit Rider: Rider Wording v1 (DUMMY)

## 1. Benefit

DUMMY: This rider, unique identification number 999A007V01, may be attached at inception to Suraksha Term Shield (999N001V02) or Suraksha Term Shield ROP (999N002V01). If the life assured dies as a direct result of an accident during the rider term, and death occurs within one hundred and eighty days of the accident, the insurer pays the rider sum assured in addition to the death benefit of the base policy. An accident is a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, violent and visible means. The rider sum assured is chosen at inception and cannot exceed the base sum assured on death. The rider term and premium paying term cannot be longer than those of the base policy, and the rider ends when the base policy ends for any reason. The rider premium is shown separately in the schedule and does not count towards any return of premium under the base policy. The rider benefit is paid once, as a lump sum, to the nominee of the base policy, and the rider then ends. The rider benefit is paid in addition to, and not instead of, the base death benefit, so on an accidental death within the period the nominee receives both amounts, while on a death from any other cause only the base death benefit is paid.

## 2. Exclusions

DUMMY: No rider benefit is paid if death is caused directly or indirectly by any of the following: intentional self-inflicted injury or suicide, whether sane or insane; the influence of alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a medical practitioner; participation in a criminal or unlawful act; war, invasion, hostilities, civil war or rebellion, whether war is declared or not; service in the armed, police or paramilitary forces in an operation; participation in hazardous sports or activities such as racing, mountaineering beyond trekking routes, parachuting, bungee jumping or scuba diving, unless declared and accepted; aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a licensed commercial airline; nuclear contamination; or a disease or infection, unless it results directly from an accidental injury. Death from illness is covered by the base policy but not by this rider. If an exclusion applies, the base policy death benefit is still payable under its own terms. Death while driving without a valid licence, or while committing a traffic offence involving alcohol, is treated as caused by the excluded activity. An exclusion applies only if the excluded cause directly or indirectly led to the death. The insurer relies on the first information report, the post-mortem report and the police final report to decide whether an exclusion applies, and gives written reasons if it does.

## 3. Waiting periods and policy conditions

DUMMY: This section sets out when the rider cover begins to respond, and the free-look, grace and revival conditions that apply to the rider.

### 3.1 Waiting and survival periods

DUMMY: This rider has no waiting period: an accident that happens on or after the date the rider starts is covered from the first day. The rider requires death to occur within one hundred and eighty days of the accident; if death occurs after that, the rider pays nothing, although the base policy pays its death benefit. The insurer may ask for evidence that the accident was the direct and sole cause of death, such as the first information report, the post-mortem report and the hospital records. If the rider has lapsed and is later revived, an accident that happened while the rider was lapsed is not covered even if death occurs after the revival. There is no survival period after diagnosis, because the rider covers death and not illness.

### 3.2 Free-look, grace and revival

DUMMY: The rider follows the policy conditions of the base policy it is attached to, with the changes set out below.

#### 3.2.1 Free-look

DUMMY: A free-look cancellation of the base policy within thirty days of receipt cancels the rider, and the rider premium is refunded on the same basis as the base premium. The rider alone may also be returned within thirty days of receipt of the rider schedule.

#### 3.2.2 Grace period

DUMMY: The rider premium is due with the base premium and has the same grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days for monthly premiums. Rider cover continues during the grace period, and an accident in the grace period is covered.

#### 3.2.3 Revival

DUMMY: The rider can be revived only together with the base policy, within five years of the first unpaid premium, by paying the overdue rider premiums with interest. The insurer may ask for evidence of good health before reviving the rider.

## 4. Surrender, termination and claims

DUMMY: This section sets out when the rider ends, what is payable when it ends, and how to make a claim under it.

### 4.1 Surrender and termination

DUMMY: This rider has no surrender value and no paid-up value, and no amount is payable if it is discontinued or surrendered. The rider ends on the earliest of the following: payment of the rider benefit; the end of the rider term; the death of the life assured from a cause other than an accident; the lapse, surrender, free-look cancellation or maturity of the base policy; the base policy becoming paid-up; or the policyholder's written request to remove the rider, effective from the next premium due date. Once the rider has ended it cannot be added again to the same base policy. The premium for this rider, together with all other non-health riders on the policy, cannot exceed thirty percent of the base premium.

### 4.2 Claims

DUMMY: A claim under this rider is intimated together with the death claim under the base policy, through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre. In addition to the documents for the base death claim, the claimant should submit the first information report or police panchnama, the post-mortem report, the final police report where available, and the hospital records if the life assured was treated before death. The insurer settles a rider claim needing no investigation within fifteen days of the last document, and completes any investigation within forty-five days of intimation. If the rider claim is repudiated because an exclusion applies, the base policy death benefit is still paid under its own terms, and the reasons for the repudiation are given in writing.
