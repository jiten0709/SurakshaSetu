# Suraksha Waiver of Premium Rider: Rider Wording v1 (DUMMY)

## 1. Benefit

DUMMY: This rider, unique identification number 999A008V01, may be attached at inception to Suraksha Term Shield (999N001V02) or Suraksha Saver Guarantee (999N010V01). If, during the rider term, the life assured suffers total and permanent disability lasting at least six months, or is first diagnosed with one of the critical illnesses listed in the annexure, all future premiums of the base policy and of every other rider are waived from the next due date, and the policy continues with full benefits as if the premiums were being paid. Total and permanent disability means the life assured is unable to do any work for pay or profit because of illness or injury, as confirmed by a medical practitioner appointed by the insurer. The rider is not available with a single premium policy, because no future premium is due. The waiver ends when the base premium paying term ends. The waiver means that the policy benefits, including any maturity benefit or guaranteed additions, continue exactly as if the policyholder were still paying. The rider premium depends on the age of the life assured, the premium paying term and the amount of premium waived, and is shown separately in the schedule. Where the proposer and the life assured are different persons, the rider covers the life assured only.

## 2. Exclusions

DUMMY: No premium is waived if the disability or critical illness is caused directly or indirectly by any of the following: intentional self-inflicted injury or attempted suicide; the influence of alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a medical practitioner; participation in a criminal act; war, invasion, civil war or rebellion; participation in hazardous sports or activities unless declared and accepted; nuclear contamination; or a condition for which the life assured had symptoms, was diagnosed or received treatment within forty-eight months before the start or revival of the rider, unless it was disclosed in the proposal and accepted by the insurer. Congenital conditions and HIV or AIDS are excluded from the critical illness part of the benefit. The exclusions of the base policy continue to apply to its own benefits. Mental illness is covered for disability only if it prevents all work for pay for at least six months and is certified by a psychiatrist appointed by the insurer. A condition present before the rider started but not known to the life assured is covered after the waiting period. Pregnancy or childbirth is not a disability under this rider unless it leads to a covered critical illness. The insurer gives written reasons whenever it relies on an exclusion.

## 3. Waiting periods and policy conditions

DUMMY: This section sets out when the rider cover begins to respond, and the free-look, grace and revival conditions that apply to the rider.

### 3.1 Waiting and survival periods

DUMMY: A waiting period of ninety days applies from the start of the rider, or from its revival, for disability or critical illness caused by sickness: a condition that first shows symptoms or is diagnosed during this period is not covered, even if it continues after the period ends. There is no waiting period for disability caused by an accident. For total and permanent disability, the disability must continue for at least six months before a claim is admitted, and premiums falling due during those six months must be paid, but are refunded without interest once the claim is admitted. For critical illness, the life assured must survive for at least thirty days after the diagnosis. The waiting period does not apply again when premiums are paid within the grace period.

### 3.2 Free-look, grace and revival

DUMMY: The rider follows the policy conditions of the base policy it is attached to, with the changes set out below.

#### 3.2.1 Free-look

DUMMY: A free-look cancellation of the base policy within thirty days of receipt cancels this rider, and its premium is refunded on the same basis as the base premium.

#### 3.2.2 Grace period

DUMMY: The rider premium is paid with the base premium and has the same grace period, thirty days or fifteen days for monthly premiums. A disability or diagnosis during the grace period is covered if the overdue premium is paid.

#### 3.2.3 Revival

DUMMY: The rider can be revived only with the base policy, within five years of the first unpaid premium. The ninety-day waiting period for sickness applies again from the date of revival.

## 4. Surrender, termination and claims

DUMMY: This section sets out when the rider ends, what is payable when it ends, and how to make a claim under it.

### 4.1 Surrender and termination

DUMMY: This rider has no surrender value and no paid-up value. It ends on the earliest of the following: the end of the base premium paying term; the lapse, surrender, free-look cancellation or paid-up conversion of the base policy; the death of the life assured; or the policyholder's written request to remove the rider from the next premium due date. Once premiums have been waived under this rider, the waiver continues until the end of the premium paying term even if the life assured recovers, and the rider cannot be removed. The premium for this rider, together with any critical illness rider on the same policy, cannot exceed one hundred percent of the base premium. A surrender of the base policy after premiums have been waived is paid on the base policy's own surrender terms.

### 4.2 Claims

DUMMY: A claim for the waiver of premiums is made through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre, with the claim form, the medical records and reports establishing the disability or the diagnosis of the critical illness, the certificate of the treating specialist, and proof of identity. For a disability claim, the insurer may ask the life assured to undergo a medical examination by a practitioner it appoints, at its own cost, and may ask for evidence that the disability continues once a year. The insurer decides a claim within fifteen days of the last document, or within forty-five days where investigation is needed. Premiums paid after the date of disability or diagnosis and before the claim is admitted are refunded.
