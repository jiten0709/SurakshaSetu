# Suraksha Term Shield: Brochure v1 (DUMMY)

## 1. Protection for the people who depend on you

DUMMY: Suraksha Term Shield is a pure protection plan that helps your family keep their home, their education plans and their everyday life going if you are no longer there to provide for them. You choose the cover, from twenty-five lakh up to ten crore rupees, and how long it lasts, from ten to forty years, so that it runs until your children are independent or your home loan is repaid. Because the plan pays only on death and has no savings element, a large cover is available for a comparatively small premium, and the premium stays the same for the whole premium paying term. Your family can receive the benefit at once or as a monthly income for ten years, which helps replace a regular salary. The plan covers death from illness, accident or any other cause from the first day, except suicide in the first year. Premiums you pay may qualify for tax deduction under the old tax regime, subject to the conditions of the income-tax law in force, which may change. Choosing the right cover matters more than choosing the lowest premium: a common guide is to cover several times your annual income plus your outstanding loans, less the savings your family can rely on. The assistant can help you estimate this need before you see any plan.

## 2. Choose the way you pay and add riders

DUMMY: You can pay premiums every year, half-year, quarter or month for the whole policy term, pay for ten years only and stay covered for the full term, or pay a single premium at the start. Limited and single premium options build a surrender value; the regular pay option has none. You can strengthen your cover with three optional riders, each with its own premium. The accidental death benefit rider pays an additional amount if death is caused by an accident. The waiver of premium rider pays your future premiums if you are totally and permanently disabled or diagnosed with a covered critical illness, so your cover continues. The critical illness rider pays a lump sum on the first diagnosis of any of the listed critical illnesses, after a ninety-day waiting period. Riders are subject to their own exclusions and waiting periods. Ask for the customer information sheet and read the policy wording before you buy; this brochure is a summary and the policy wording prevails. Premiums can be paid yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly, through auto-debit, card or net banking. You have a grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days for monthly premiums, to pay each premium, and thirty days after receiving the policy to return it if you change your mind.
