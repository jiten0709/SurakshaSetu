# Suraksha Saver Guarantee: Policy Wording v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Definitions

DUMMY: In this policy, the life assured is the person whose life is insured and the policyholder is the owner of the policy. The sum assured on death is chosen at inception between five lakh rupees and one crore rupees. The policy term is chosen between ten and twenty-five years, and the policy matures no later than age eighty of the life assured. The entry age is between eighteen and fifty-five years. Premiums are payable for ten years only, which is the limited premium paying term of this plan. The guaranteed maturity benefit is the amount stated in the schedule, fixed at inception, that is payable on survival to the maturity date. Guaranteed additions are amounts added to the policy at the end of each policy year while premiums are paid, at the rate shown in the schedule. The benefit illustration is the document, given before the proposal, that shows the guaranteed benefits of the plan; this plan has no non-guaranteed benefits. Total premiums paid The annualised premium is the premium payable in a year, excluding taxes, rider premiums, underwriting extra premiums and modal loadings. The paid-up value is the reduced death and maturity benefit that continues after premiums stop, once the policy has acquired a surrender value. The schedule records the life assured, the sum assured, the guaranteed maturity benefit and the rate of guaranteed additions. excludes taxes, rider premiums and extra premiums.

## 2. Benefits

DUMMY: This is a non-linked, non-participating savings plan. All its benefits are guaranteed at inception and do not depend on investment performance or bonus declarations.

### 2.1 Death benefit

DUMMY: On the death of the life assured during the policy term while the policy is in force, the death benefit is the sum assured on death plus the guaranteed additions accrued up to the date of death, subject to a minimum of one hundred and five percent of the total premiums paid. The sum assured on death is the higher of the basic sum assured and ten times the annualised premium. The benefit is paid to the nominee, and the policy ends.

### 2.2 Maturity benefit and income option

DUMMY: If the life assured survives to the maturity date with the policy in force, the insurer pays the guaranteed maturity benefit plus the accrued guaranteed additions. At inception, the policyholder chooses to receive the maturity benefit as a lump sum or as a monthly income for five years, in which case the monthly amount is fixed in the schedule and the total income is higher than the lump sum because of the deferred payment. The option cannot be changed after inception. The guaranteed additions for each policy year are shown in the benefit illustration, and the policyholder receives an annual statement of the additions credited to date.

## 3. Exclusions and waiting periods

DUMMY: The benefits of this policy are subject to the exclusions and waiting periods in this section. No other exclusion or waiting period applies to the base cover, and each rider has its own exclusions and waiting periods set out in its rider wording.

### 3.1 Exclusions

DUMMY: If the life assured dies by suicide within twelve months from the date of commencement of risk or revival, the nominee receives the higher of eighty percent of the total premiums paid and the surrender value on the date of death, provided the policy is in force. No other exclusion applies to the death benefit of this plan. The waiver of premium rider, which is the only rider available with this plan, has its own exclusions for disability or illness caused by self-inflicted injury, war, criminal acts, or conditions existing before the rider started, as set out in its rider wording. The maturity benefit is not subject to any exclusion, but it is payable only if the policy is in force on the maturity date. This product is part of the seed catalog but is not offered for sale in phase 1 of the assistant.

### 3.2 Waiting periods

DUMMY: The death benefit of this plan has no waiting period: death from any cause other than suicide is covered from the date of commencement of risk. The twelve-month suicide provision applies from commencement or revival. Guaranteed additions accrue only at the end of each completed policy year for which the premium has been paid, so no addition is credited during the first policy year. The waiver of premium rider applies a waiting period of ninety days from commencement or revival for disability or critical illness caused by sickness, and no waiting period for disability caused by an accident. A claim under the waiver of premium rider also requires the disability to last at least six months before premiums are waived. Revival restarts the suicide period and the rider's illness waiting period from the date of revival.

## 4. Free-look and grace period

DUMMY: This section sets out the policyholder's right to return the policy after receiving it, and the time allowed to pay each premium after its due date.

### 4.1 Free-look period

DUMMY: The policyholder may return the policy within thirty days from the date of receipt of the policy document if they disagree with any of its terms, stating the objection in writing or through the website or app. The insurer will refund the premium paid within seven days, after deducting the proportionate risk premium for the period of cover, stamp duty and any medical examination costs. Guaranteed additions do not accrue on a policy cancelled during free-look, and no surrender charge applies. For an electronic policy, the free-look period starts on delivery to the policyholder's registered email or account. The benefit illustration acknowledged at the proposal stage is attached to the policy document so that the policyholder can compare the benefits with what was illustrated before deciding.

### 4.2 Grace period

DUMMY: A grace period of thirty days is allowed after the due date of each yearly, half-yearly or quarterly premium, and fifteen days for each monthly premium. The policy stays in force with full benefits during the grace period, and if the life assured dies during it, the death benefit is paid after deducting the unpaid premium. If a premium is not paid by the end of the grace period before two full years of premiums have been paid, the policy lapses and no benefit is payable. If two full years of premiums have been paid, the policy does not lapse but becomes paid-up under section 5.2, and no further guaranteed additions accrue. Premium reminders are sent before each due date.

## 5. Revival and surrender

DUMMY: This section sets out how a lapsed or paid-up policy can be restored, and what the policyholder receives if the policy is surrendered or becomes paid-up.

### 5.1 Revival

DUMMY: A lapsed or paid-up policy may be revived within five years from the due date of the first unpaid premium and before the maturity date, by paying all overdue premiums with interest at the revival rate declared by the insurer and providing evidence of insurability if the insurer asks for it. On revival, the full sum assured, the guaranteed maturity benefit and the accrual of guaranteed additions are restored. Guaranteed additions that did not accrue while the policy was lapsed or paid-up are credited on revival only for the years whose premiums are paid on revival. The insurer may decline a revival, with reasons, and the suicide provision applies again from the date of revival.

### 5.2 Surrender and paid-up value

DUMMY: The policy acquires a guaranteed surrender value after two full years of premiums have been paid, as required for savings products. The surrender value is the higher of the guaranteed surrender value, which is a percentage of the total premiums paid plus a percentage of the accrued guaranteed additions, and the special surrender value, using the factors in the annexure filed with the Authority. If premiums stop after two years, the policy becomes paid-up: the death and maturity benefits are reduced in proportion to the premiums paid out of the premiums payable, and the guaranteed additions already accrued are kept. The surrender value is paid within seven days of a complete request, and the policy ends on surrender.

## 6. Claims and policy conditions

DUMMY: This section sets out how to make a claim and the legal conditions that apply to the policy throughout its term.

### 6.1 Claims

DUMMY: A death claim is intimated by the nominee or legal heir through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre, with the claim form, the death certificate, the policy document, proof of identity and bank details of the claimant, and police and post-mortem reports for an unnatural death. A claim within three years of commencement or revival may be investigated. A claim needing no investigation is settled within fifteen days of the last document, and any investigation is completed within forty-five days of intimation. The maturity benefit or the first monthly income is paid on the maturity date; the insurer asks for bank details and proof of identity at least sixty days before. Late payment carries interest at two percent above the bank rate.

### 6.2 Misstatement, nomination, assignment and loans

DUMMY: Under section 45 of the Insurance Act, the policy cannot be called in question on any ground after three years from the later of the date of issue, commencement of risk or revival; within three years it may be repudiated for fraud or for misstatement or suppression of a material fact, with written reasons. Nomination under section 39 and assignment under section 38 of the Insurance Act are available, and an assignment cancels any nomination. Once the policy has acquired a surrender value, the policyholder may take a loan of up to seventy percent of the surrender value at the loan interest rate declared by the insurer; an unpaid loan with interest is deducted from any benefit, and the policy is foreclosed if the outstanding loan exceeds the surrender value.
