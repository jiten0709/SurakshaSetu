# Suraksha Term Shield ROP: Customer Information Sheet v2 (DUMMY)

## 1. Key features

DUMMY: Suraksha Term Shield ROP, unique identification number 999N002V01, is a non-linked, non-participating individual life insurance plan that pays a death benefit during the policy term and returns the premiums paid if the life assured survives to the end of the term. The entry age is eighteen to fifty-five years, cover ends by age seventy-five at the latest, and the policy term is fifteen to thirty-five years (clause 1). The sum assured on death is between twenty-five lakh and two crore rupees. Premiums are payable for the whole term (regular pay) or for ten years (limited pay). The death benefit is paid as a lump sum. The only rider available is the accidental death benefit rider 999A007V01. Because part of the premium funds the return of premium, the premium for this plan is higher than for a term plan with the same cover and no return of premium. Premiums shown before underwriting are indicative only. Premiums can be paid yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly, and modal factors apply to non-annual premiums. If you want to compare the cost of the return of premium feature, ask for the premium for the same cover under Suraksha Term Shield, which has no return of premium.

## 2. Benefits and exclusions in brief

DUMMY: On death during the policy term, the nominee receives the highest of the sum assured on death, ten times the annualised premium and one hundred and five percent of the premiums paid (clause 2.1). On survival to the maturity date with all premiums paid, the policy returns one hundred percent of the total premiums paid, excluding taxes, rider premiums and extra premiums (clause 2.2); the returned amount is therefore less than everything you pay. The only exclusion on the base cover is suicide within twelve months of the start or revival, when eighty percent of the premiums paid or the surrender value, whichever is higher, is paid (clause 3.1). The base death benefit has no waiting period (clause 3.2). Non-disclosure of a material fact may lead to repudiation within three years under section 45 of the Insurance Act (clause 6.2). The accidental death benefit rider, if chosen, pays an additional amount on accidental death within one hundred and eighty days of an accident, and has its own exclusions, such as hazardous sports and the influence of alcohol. There is no loan facility under this plan, and no bonus is payable because it is a non-participating plan.

## 3. Your rights, service and claims

DUMMY: You may return the policy within thirty days of receiving it if you disagree with its terms, and the premium is refunded after small deductions (clause 4.1). Each premium has a grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days if paid monthly (clause 4.2). If premiums stop in the first two years the policy lapses; after two full years it becomes paid-up with reduced cover and a reduced return of premium (clauses 4.2 and 5.2). A lapsed or paid-up policy may be revived within five years (clause 5.1). The policy acquires a surrender value after two full years of premiums (clause 5.2). Claims are made through any branch, the website, the app or the call centre and settled within fifteen days of the last document when no investigation is needed (clause 6.1). Complaints go to the grievance cell, then the Grievance Redressal Officer, Bima Bharosa or the Insurance Ombudsman. Surrendering in the early years returns much less than the premiums paid. Keep the policy document and premium receipts safe, and update your nominee and contact details through the app or a branch whenever they change.
