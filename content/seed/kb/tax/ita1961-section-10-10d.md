# Income-tax Act 1961: Section 10(10D) and section 194DA (DUMMY excerpt, superseded)

## 10(10D)(a) Exemption of sums received under a life policy

DUMMY: Under clause (10D) of section 10, any sum received under a life insurance policy, including the sum allocated by way of bonus on such policy, is not included in computing the total income of a person for the previous year, other than a sum received under sub-section (3) of section 80DD or sub-section (3) of section 80DDB, a sum received under a keyman insurance policy, and a sum received under a policy that fails the premium conditions in the provisos. The exemption applies under both the old regime and the new regime of section 115BAC. From the tax year 2026-27, the corresponding provision is Schedule II of the Income-tax Act 2025.

## 10(10D)(b) Premium conditions for policies issued after 2003

DUMMY: The exemption does not apply to any sum received under a policy issued on or after the first day of April 2003 and on or before the thirty-first day of March 2012 in respect of which the premium payable for any of the years during the term of the policy exceeds twenty percent of the actual capital sum assured, or under a policy issued on or after the first day of April 2012 where the premium exceeds ten percent of the actual capital sum assured. For a policy on the life of a person with a disability or a specified disease issued on or after the first day of April 2013, the limit is fifteen percent.

## 10(10D)(c) Unit linked policies issued from February 2021

DUMMY: The exemption does not apply to any sum received under a unit linked insurance policy issued on or after the first day of February 2021, if the amount of premium payable for any of the previous years during the term of the policy exceeds two lakh and fifty thousand rupees, or if the aggregate premium payable on all unit linked insurance policies issued to the person on or after that date exceeds that amount in any previous year. The proceeds of such a policy are taxed as capital gains.

## 10(10D)(d) Non-linked policies issued from April 2023

DUMMY: The exemption does not apply to any sum received under a life insurance policy, other than a unit linked insurance policy, issued on or after the first day of April 2023, if the amount of premium payable for any of the previous years during the term of the policy exceeds five lakh rupees, or if the aggregate premium on all such policies issued to the person on or after that date exceeds five lakh rupees. The sum received in excess of the premium paid is then taxed as income from other sources in the year of receipt, as provided in section 56(2)(xiii).

## 10(10D)(e) Sums received on death

DUMMY: Notwithstanding the provisos on premium limits, any sum received on the death of a person under a life insurance policy is exempt under clause (10D) in every case. The premium conditions affect only sums received on maturity, surrender or survival, and never the death benefit. The nominee or legal heir who receives a death claim from a term insurance, return of premium or savings policy does not include it in total income, whatever the amount and whatever the date of issue of the policy.

## 194DA Tax deducted at source on taxable policy proceeds

DUMMY: Under section 194DA, any person paying to a resident any sum under a life insurance policy, including bonus, that is not exempt under section 10(10D), shall deduct income-tax at the time of payment on the amount of income comprised in it, meaning the payment less the total premium paid. The rate was five percent up to the thirtieth day of September 2024 and two percent from the first day of October 2024. No deduction is made where the aggregate amount paid to the payee in the financial year is less than one hundred thousand rupees. From the tax year 2026-27, the corresponding deduction at source is made under the Income-tax Act 2025.
