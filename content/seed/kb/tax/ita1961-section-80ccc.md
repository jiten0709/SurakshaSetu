# Income-tax Act 1961: Section 80CCC (DUMMY excerpt, superseded)

## 80CCC(1) Contribution to pension funds of insurers

DUMMY: Where an assessee, being an individual, has in the previous year paid or deposited any amount out of income chargeable to tax to effect or keep in force a contract for any annuity plan of an insurer for receiving a pension from a fund set up by the insurer, the assessee is allowed a deduction of the amount so paid, up to one lakh and fifty thousand rupees, subject to the aggregate limit of section 80CCE. The deduction is available only to individuals, not to Hindu undivided families, and only under the old regime. Interest or bonus accrued or credited to the assessee's account under the plan is not counted as a contribution. From the tax year 2026-27, the corresponding provision is paragraph 3 of Schedule XV of the Income-tax Act 2025.

## 80CCC(2) Taxation of pension and surrender proceeds

DUMMY: Where any amount standing to the credit of the assessee in the pension fund, for which a deduction has been allowed under sub-section (1), together with interest or bonus accrued, is received by the assessee or the nominee on account of the surrender of the annuity plan, in whole or in part, in any previous year, or as a pension received from the annuity plan, that amount is deemed to be the income of the assessee or the nominee in the year of receipt and is chargeable to tax. The commuted part of a pension that is exempt under any other provision is not affected by this sub-section.

## 80CCC(3) No double deduction

DUMMY: Where any amount paid or deposited by the assessee has been taken into account for a deduction under this section, a rebate or deduction for the same amount shall not be allowed under section 80C. The same payment therefore cannot be claimed twice, once as a pension contribution and once as a life insurance premium. The aggregate of deductions under sections 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD(1) remains subject to the combined limit of one lakh and fifty thousand rupees in section 80CCE. An assessee who pays tax under the new regime of section 115BAC is not allowed this deduction at all.
