# Exposure Draft on Conversational AI in Insurance Distribution (DUMMY, pending review)

## 1. Proposal

DUMMY: This fictitious exposure draft proposes that every insurer using a conversational AI system to sell insurance shall register the system with the Authority before use, and shall impose a mandatory cooling-off period of forty-eight hours between an AI-assisted recommendation and the acceptance of the proposal. During the cooling-off period the prospect would receive a summary of the recommendation and the customer information sheet by email. The draft also proposes a quarterly independent audit of every registered system, covering bias, accuracy of product information and the handling of vulnerable customers. The draft is open for comments and has no legal force.

## 2. Comments invited

DUMMY: Stakeholders are invited to comment on the proposed registration process, the length of the cooling-off period, the scope of the quarterly audit and the transition period for systems already in use. Comments are to be sent within thirty days of the date of the draft in the format given in the annexure. The Authority may modify the proposals in the light of the comments received before issuing any final regulation.
