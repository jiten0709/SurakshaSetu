# Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024 (DUMMY excerpt)

## 1. Scope and product categories

DUMMY: This fictitious master circular sets the design and disclosure standards for every individual life insurance product filed with the Authority. Products are classified as linked or non-linked, and non-linked products as participating or non-participating. Pure protection products, including term insurance with or without return of premium, provide a benefit only on death or on a specified health event, while savings products provide a benefit on survival to maturity as well. Every product and every rider carries a unique identification number issued by the Authority, which shall appear on the policy document, the customer information sheet, the benefit illustration, the brochure and every sales communication. A product may be sold only in the form in which it was filed, and any change to its terms requires a fresh filing under a new version of the identification number.

## 2. Customer information sheet and benefit illustration

DUMMY: Chapter 2 prescribes the two documents that every prospect receives before buying: the customer information sheet and, for savings products, the benefit illustration.

### 2.1 Format of the customer information sheet

DUMMY: The customer information sheet shall follow the tabular format in the annexure to this circular, with one row for each of the following: product name and identification number, policy term, premium paying term, sum assured, death benefit, maturity or survival benefit, riders, free-look period, grace period, revival, surrender, exclusions, waiting periods, claim procedure and grievance contacts. Each row shall refer to the clause of the policy wording where the full terms appear. The sheet shall use the same terms as the policy wording and shall not introduce any new condition.

### 2.2 Benefit illustration

DUMMY: A benefit illustration shall be given for every savings product and for every product with a survival benefit. Guaranteed benefits shall be shown separately from non-guaranteed benefits. Non-guaranteed benefits of participating and linked products shall be illustrated at two assumed rates of return, four percent and eight percent a year, and the illustration shall state that these are not the upper or lower limits of what may be received. The illustration shall be signed or acknowledged by the prospect, and a copy shall be attached to the policy document.

Explanation.— A pure protection product that pays no benefit on survival does not require a benefit illustration, but its customer information sheet shall state the premium for the chosen sum assured and term.

## 3. Standard policy conditions

DUMMY: Chapter 3 lists the conditions that every individual life policy shall carry.

### 3.1 Free-look period

DUMMY: Every individual life policy shall carry a free-look period of thirty days from the date of receipt of the policy document, during which the policyholder may return the policy stating any objection to its terms. The policy wording shall set out the free-look refund in the manner specified in the master circular on protection of policyholders' interests.

### 3.2 Grace period

DUMMY: A grace period of not less than thirty days shall be allowed for yearly, half-yearly and quarterly premiums, and not less than fifteen days for monthly premiums. The policy remains in force with full cover during the grace period. If the life assured dies during the grace period, the insurer shall pay the death benefit after deducting the unpaid premium. A policy whose premium remains unpaid at the end of the grace period lapses or becomes paid-up according to its terms.

### 3.3 Revival

DUMMY: A lapsed or paid-up policy may be revived within five years from the date of the first unpaid premium, or such longer period as the product allows, on payment of all arrears of premium with interest at the rate stated in the policy and on proof of continued insurability where the insurer requires it. A revival restores all benefits from the date of revival.

### 3.4 Surrender value

DUMMY: Every savings product shall acquire a guaranteed surrender value after the policyholder has paid premiums for two full years, and a special surrender value where the product's filing provides one. A regular-premium pure protection product need not acquire a surrender value; a limited or single-premium protection product shall acquire one as filed. The surrender value basis shall be disclosed in the policy wording and the customer information sheet.

## 4. Riders

DUMMY: A rider is an add-on benefit that attaches to a base policy and carries its own identification number. The premium for all health-related and critical illness riders together shall not exceed one hundred percent of the premium of the base product, and the premium for all other riders together shall not exceed thirty percent of the base premium. The rider benefit shall not exceed the sum assured of the base policy. A rider shall end no later than the base policy, and the lapse, surrender or free-look cancellation of the base policy ends every rider attached to it. The customer information sheet shall show each rider with its own premium, benefit and exclusions.

## 5. Sale of pure protection products

DUMMY: A pure protection product shall be offered with a clear statement that no benefit is payable on survival to the end of the policy term, except where the product returns premiums on survival. The distributor shall explain the difference between cover sized to the proposer's needs and cover sized to the premium the proposer wishes to pay. Where the proposer chooses cover lower than the assessed need, the recommendation record shall show the assessed need, the cover chosen and the protection gap. A term product with a return of premium feature shall state that the returned amount excludes taxes, rider premiums and any extra premium charged for underwriting.

## 6. Communications and advertisements

DUMMY: Every advertisement and sales communication shall name the product and its identification number, shall be consistent with the filed product, and shall not highlight a benefit without also stating the main conditions and exclusions that apply to it. Illustrations used in advertisements shall follow the benefit illustration rules. Claims about tax benefits shall state that the benefits are subject to the conditions of the income-tax law in force and may change. Automated and conversational channels are sales communications under this chapter, and their scripts shall be approved and versioned in the same way as printed material.
