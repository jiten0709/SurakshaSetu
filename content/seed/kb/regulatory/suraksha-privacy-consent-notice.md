# Privacy and Consent Notice (DUMMY)

## 1. Who we are and what this notice covers

DUMMY: This notice is issued by Suraksha Life, a fictitious life insurer used only to test this assistant, as the data fiduciary for the personal data you share while using the insurance assistant on our website or app. It explains what we collect, why we collect it, the purposes you can agree to, how long we keep your data, and the rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. The assistant is an automated system operated by us; it is not a person, and you can ask to speak to a licensed human advisor at any time. The notice applies from the notice version 2026.09.1 and is available in English and Hindi. When the notice changes, we will show you the new version and ask for your consent again before using your data for any new purpose.

## 2. The purposes you can agree to

DUMMY: We ask for your consent separately for each purpose, and you can choose each one independently.

### 2.1 P1: Assess your needs and suggest plans

DUMMY: Purpose P1 is required to use the assistant. With it, we use your answers, including basic health and lifestyle details such as tobacco use, to check your eligibility, assess your insurance needs and suggest suitable plans with indicative premiums. Without P1 the assistant cannot ask you questions about yourself or suggest a plan, but you can still read general information and contact a branch or our helpline. P1 does not allow us to contact you later or to send you marketing.

### 2.2 P2: Let a licensed advisor contact you

DUMMY: Purpose P2 is optional. With it, a licensed advisor may contact you by phone, email or message about the needs assessment you completed, using the contact details you give us. The advisor sees a summary of your answers so that you do not have to repeat them. If you do not give P2, no advisor will contact you, and you can still ask for a call back during the conversation.

### 2.3 P3: Send you product updates

DUMMY: Purpose P3 is optional. With it, we may send you information about our products and services. We will not share your data with any other company for its marketing. You can turn off P3 at any time from the link in each message or by telling the assistant, and turning it off does not affect P1 or P2.

## 3. Age declaration

DUMMY: The assistant is meant only for adults. Before we collect any personal data, we ask you to confirm that you are eighteen years of age or older. If you tell us, or your answers show, that you are under eighteen, the assistant stops collecting data, erases what you have already told us in that conversation, and suggests that a parent or guardian contact a branch or our helpline. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children through the assistant, and we do not track or profile any user who declares they are under eighteen.

## 4. Withdrawing consent

DUMMY: You can withdraw your consent for any purpose at any time, as easily as you gave it, by telling the assistant that you withdraw consent, by using the withdraw option on the screen, or by writing to our grievance officer. When you withdraw P1, the assistant stops asking questions and stops using your answers in the same turn, and no further suggestion is made. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it, and we may keep records that the law requires us to keep, such as the record of your consent itself and any sale that was completed.

## 5. Retention and erasure

DUMMY: We keep the answers you give the assistant only as long as needed for the purposes you agreed to, and in any case not longer than thirty days after your last activity unless you buy a policy or the law requires longer retention. You can ask us to erase your data at any time. We protect your data with encryption under a key that is unique to you, and erasure destroys that key, so the encrypted data can no longer be read by anyone. Records we must keep by law, such as audit records of what the assistant showed you, keep only the minimum needed and never store your answers in readable form.

## 6. Your rights and how to complain

DUMMY: Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act you have the right to access a summary of the personal data we hold about you, to correct or complete it, to have it erased, to nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity, and to have your grievance addressed. You can exercise these rights through the assistant, our website or by writing to the grievance officer named in our grievance redressal policy. We will respond within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
