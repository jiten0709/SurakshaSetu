# Insurance Act 1938 (DUMMY excerpt)

## 38. Assignment and transfer of policies

DUMMY: A transfer or assignment of a policy of insurance, wholly or in part, whether with or without consideration, may be made only by an endorsement upon the policy itself or by a separate instrument, signed by the transferor or assignor or their duly authorised agent and attested by at least one witness, specifically setting forth the fact of transfer or assignment and the reasons for it. The assignment is complete and effectual on execution, but is not operative as against the insurer until notice in writing of the assignment, with the instrument or a certified copy, is delivered to the insurer. The insurer may decline to act upon an assignment where it has sufficient reason to believe that it is not bona fide or not in the interest of the policyholder or public interest, with written reasons. An assignment of a policy cancels any nomination made under it, except an assignment to the insurer against a loan.

## 39. Nomination by the policyholder

DUMMY: The holder of a policy of life insurance on their own life may, when effecting the policy or at any time before the policy matures for payment, nominate one or more persons to whom the money secured by the policy shall be paid in the event of their death. Where a nominee is a minor, the policyholder may appoint a person to receive the money during the minority of the nominee. A nomination may be cancelled or changed at any time before the policy matures by an endorsement or a further endorsement or a will, but the insurer is not liable for a payment made bona fide before it receives notice of the change. Where the nominee is the parent, spouse or child of the policyholder, the nominee is the beneficial owner of the amount payable, unless the policyholder specifies otherwise.

## 41. Prohibition of rebates

DUMMY: No person shall allow or offer to allow, either directly or indirectly, as an inducement to any person to take out or renew or continue an insurance in respect of any kind of risk relating to lives or property in India, any rebate of the whole or part of the commission payable or any rebate of the premium shown on the policy, nor shall any person taking out or renewing or continuing a policy accept any rebate, except such rebate as may be allowed in accordance with the published prospectuses or tables of the insurer. Any person making default in complying with this section is liable to a penalty which may extend to ten lakh rupees.

## 45. Policy not to be called in question after three years

DUMMY: No policy of life insurance shall be called in question on any ground whatsoever after the expiry of three years from the date of the policy, that is, from the date of issuance of the policy or the date of commencement of risk or the date of revival of the policy or the date of the rider to the policy, whichever is later. A policy may be called in question within three years on the ground of fraud, or on the ground that any statement of or suppression of a fact material to the expectancy of the life of the insured was incorrectly made in the proposal or other document, with the insurer communicating in writing the grounds and materials on which the decision is based. A mere inaccurate or false statement is not fraud unless it was made knowingly, and the burden of proving that the policyholder knew of the suppressed fact lies on the insurer.

## 64VB. No risk to be assumed unless premium is received in advance

DUMMY: No insurer shall assume any risk in India in respect of any insurance business on which premium is ordinarily payable in India unless and until the premium payable is received by it or is guaranteed to be paid by such person in such manner and within such time as may be prescribed, or unless and until deposit of such amount as may be prescribed is made in advance in the prescribed manner. Any refund of premium which may become due to an insured on account of the cancellation of a policy or alteration in its terms and conditions shall be paid by the insurer directly to the insured by a crossed or order cheque or by postal money order or through electronic transfer, and a proper receipt shall be obtained.
