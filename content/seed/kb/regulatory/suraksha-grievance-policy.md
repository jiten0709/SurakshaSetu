# Grievance Redressal Policy (DUMMY)

## 1. Purpose and scope

DUMMY: This fictitious board-approved policy sets out how Suraksha Life receives, records, resolves and escalates complaints from prospects, policyholders, nominees and claimants. It covers complaints about sales, including conversations with the automated insurance assistant, policy servicing, premium collection, claims and the handling of personal data. A grievance is any communication that expresses dissatisfaction with an action or lack of action of the insurer or its distributors and seeks a remedy. A query or a request for information is not a grievance, but the staff handling it shall treat it as one if the customer says they are dissatisfied.

## 2. How to register a complaint

DUMMY: A complaint may be registered in any of the following ways, and the customer may choose the one most convenient to them: by telling the insurance assistant or a human advisor that they wish to complain, by calling the toll-free helpline, by email to the grievance cell, through the complaint form on the website, in person or in writing at any branch, or through the Authority's Bima Bharosa portal. The insurer shall not require the customer to use a particular channel and shall not ask for any document that is not needed to understand the complaint. Every complaint receives a unique reference number at registration, which is shared with the customer immediately.

## 3. Acknowledgment and resolution timelines

DUMMY: Every complaint shall be acknowledged within three days of receipt, with the reference number, the name of the officer handling it and the expected date of resolution. The insurer shall resolve the complaint and send a written reply within fourteen days of receipt. Where the complaint concerns a claim that is under investigation, the reply shall state the stage of the investigation and the date by which a decision will be made. A complaint is closed when the complainant accepts the reply, or when eight weeks pass after the final reply without a response from the complainant.

Provided that a complaint alleging mis-selling by the automated assistant shall be reviewed by a person who was not involved in approving the assistant's scripts, and the review shall consider the audit record of the conversation.

## 4. Escalation within the insurer

DUMMY: A complainant who is not satisfied with the reply, or who receives no reply within fourteen days, may escalate the complaint to the Grievance Redressal Officer at the head office, whose contact details appear in every policy document, on the website and in every reply to a complaint. The Grievance Redressal Officer shall review the complaint afresh and reply within ten days of the escalation. Complaints that remain unresolved are reported to the board's policyholder protection committee every quarter, together with an analysis of their root causes.

## 5. Insurance Ombudsman and the Authority

DUMMY: If the complaint is rejected, not resolved to the complainant's satisfaction, or not answered within thirty days, the complainant may approach the Insurance Ombudsman with jurisdiction over their address, within one year of the insurer's final reply. The Ombudsman's services are free of charge, and an award of the Ombudsman is binding on the insurer. The complainant may also register or track the complaint on the Authority's Bima Bharosa portal at any stage. Every reply sent by the insurer shall state these options and the time limits that apply to them.

## 6. Records, reporting and review

DUMMY: The grievance cell shall keep a record of every complaint, the actions taken and the final outcome for at least ten years. Personal data in complaint records is protected in the same way as other customer data, and complaint records are not used for marketing. The cell shall report the number, nature and ageing of complaints to senior management every month and to the Authority as required. The board shall review this policy at least once a year and after any significant change in regulation, products or sales channels, including the introduction of a new automated channel.
