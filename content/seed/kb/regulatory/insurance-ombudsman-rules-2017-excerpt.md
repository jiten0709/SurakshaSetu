# Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017 (DUMMY excerpt)

## 13. Complaints the Ombudsman receives

DUMMY: The Insurance Ombudsman shall receive and consider complaints or disputes relating to delay in settlement of claims beyond the time specified in the regulations, any partial or total repudiation of claims by the insurer, disputes over premium paid or payable in terms of the policy, misrepresentation of policy terms and conditions at any time in the policy document or policy contract, legal construction of insurance policies in so far as the dispute relates to a claim, policy servicing related grievances against insurers and their agents and intermediaries, issuance of a life insurance policy that is not in conformity with the proposal form submitted by the proposer, and non-issuance of an insurance policy after receipt of premium. The Ombudsman also acts as counsellor and mediator where the parties agree in writing.

## 14. Manner of lodging a complaint

DUMMY: Any person who has a grievance against an insurer may, personally or through a legal heir, nominee or assignee, make a complaint in writing to the Insurance Ombudsman within whose territorial jurisdiction the branch or office of the insurer complained against, or the residential address of the complainant, is located. The complaint may be made online. No complaint shall lie unless the complainant made a written representation to the insurer and the insurer rejected the complaint, did not reply within thirty days of receipt, or the complainant is not satisfied with the reply. The complaint shall be made within one year of the rejection or final reply of the insurer, or within one year and thirty days of the representation where the insurer did not reply, and shall not be on a matter pending before any court, consumer forum or arbitrator.

## 16. Mediation and recommendation

DUMMY: Where a complaint is settled through mediation, the Insurance Ombudsman shall make a recommendation which the Ombudsman thinks fair in the circumstances of the case, within one month of receipt of the complaint, and send copies of the recommendation to the complainant and the insurer. Where the complainant accepts the recommendation, they shall send a communication in writing within fifteen days of receipt of the recommendation stating that they accept it as a full and final settlement, and the insurer shall comply with the recommendation within fifteen days of receiving that acceptance and inform the Ombudsman of the compliance.

## 17. Award by the Insurance Ombudsman

DUMMY: Where a complaint is not settled by mediation, the Insurance Ombudsman shall pass an award within three months of receipt of all the requirements from the complainant, which shall state the amount awarded to the complainant. The Ombudsman may award compensation not exceeding fifty lakh rupees, including relevant expenses, if any. The award shall be binding on the insurer, and the insurer shall comply with the award within thirty days of the receipt of the award and intimate compliance to the Ombudsman. The complainant is not bound by the award and remains free to pursue other remedies. The services of the Insurance Ombudsman are free of charge to the complainant.

## 18. Council for Insurance Ombudsmen

DUMMY: The Council for Insurance Ombudsmen administers the offices of the Insurance Ombudsmen, maintains the list of their territorial jurisdictions and addresses, and publishes an annual report of complaints received and disposed of, classified by insurer and by the nature of the complaint. Every insurer shall display the address of the Insurance Ombudsman with jurisdiction over the policyholder in the policy document and on its website, and shall mention in every rejection of a grievance that the complainant may approach the Insurance Ombudsman within one year. An insurer that fails to comply with an award within the time specified shall be reported to the Authority for action.
